Ink & Play

A sample puzzle book that actually works on e-ink. No timers. No colour tricks. Just six turn-based games on two pages — English Games and 日本語学習ゲーム — with big tap targets, solid black-and-white contrast, and almost no screen redraws — so the Scribe stays crisp and ghost-free.

✓ E-ink first

Bold outlines and high contrast that stay clear on e-ink. Correct answers are told apart by fill and pattern, not by colour alone.

✓ Stylus friendly

Every button is at least 50px tall. Works with a finger tap or the Premium Pen.

✓ No flicker

No auto-refresh or video. The page only changes when you tap — plus one gentle bounce & wiggle celebration if you win Beetle Quest.
