Ink & Play

A sample puzzle book that actually works on e-ink. No timers. No colour tricks. Just four turn-based games with big tap targets, solid black-and-white contrast, and almost no screen redraws — so the Scribe stays crisp and ghost-free.

✓ E-ink first

Black, white & grey only. Correct answers are told apart by fill and pattern, not colour.

✓ Stylus friendly

Every button is at least 50px tall. Works with a finger tap or the Premium Pen.

✓ No flicker

No animations, no auto-refresh, no video. The page only changes when you tap.

How to open this on your Scribe

  1. Open this page on your phone / computer first — you’re already here.
  2. On the Scribe, open the Web Browser from the Home / More menu.
  3. Type this page’s address, or send it to your Kindle via Send to Kindle and open the link.
  4. Bookmark it — the games work from the cached page and need no account.

Why this sample works on e-ink

  • Turn-based only — nothing moves until you tap, so there’s no blur or battery drain.
  • Plain HTML + tap buttons — no canvas, no heavy scripts, so the Scribe’s limited browser can run it.
  • Patterns, not colour — solid, double-border and grey read clearly in greyscale.
  • One page, four games — switching games doesn’t reload the page.
